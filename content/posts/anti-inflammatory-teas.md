Inflammation is your body's natural response to injury and infection. Problems arise when it lingers at a low level for a long time. Diet and lifestyle matter most here, but some herbs contain compounds that are being studied for their anti-inflammatory effects.

## 1. Turmeric
Curcumin, the yellow pigment, is one of the most studied plant compounds. It's poorly absorbed on its own, so pair turmeric with **black pepper** and a little **fat**, such as milk.

![Turmeric tea with lemon](/uploads/2022/08/depositphotos_86528910-stock-photo-turmeric-with-lemon-tea.webp)

## 2. Ginger
A close relative of turmeric. Gingerols have shown anti-inflammatory activity in lab studies, and ginger may ease muscle soreness.

## 3. Green tea
It's rich in EGCG, a catechin with antioxidant and anti-inflammatory properties. It contains some caffeine.

## 4. Rosehip
Rosehip powder has been studied for joint comfort. [Read our rosehip guide](/rosehip-tea-amazing-benefits).

## 5. Chamomile
Chamomile contains apigenin and other flavonoids, and it's gentle enough for daily use.

## Golden milk recipe

Warm 1 cup of milk (dairy or plant) with:

- ½ teaspoon of turmeric
- ¼ teaspoon of ground ginger
- a pinch of black pepper
- a pinch of cinnamon
- honey to taste

## Keep it in perspective

These teas are a pleasant addition to a balanced lifestyle, not a treatment for inflammatory conditions. Turmeric supplements can interact with **blood thinners** and **diabetes medications**.
