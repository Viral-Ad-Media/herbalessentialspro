Your liver, kidneys, gut and lungs remove waste around the clock. No tea "detoxes" you better than they already do. So what are detox teas actually doing?

## What's often inside

**Laxatives.** Many slimming and detox blends contain **senna** or **cascara**. They make you go to the toilet more, which can make your stomach look flatter, but you're losing water and stool, not fat.

**Diuretics.** Dandelion, nettle and caffeine make you urinate more, which means you're losing water weight that comes straight back.

**Caffeine.** Green tea or guarana can suppress appetite a little and give you a lift.

## The risks of overdoing it

Using laxative teas regularly can cause **cramping, diarrhoea, dehydration and electrolyte imbalances**. Over time, it can make your bowel dependent on them. They can also stop medicines, including the **contraceptive pill**, from being absorbed properly.

## A gentler "reset"

If you want to feel lighter and fresher, try these instead:

- Drink plenty of water and caffeine-free herbal tea.
- Choose **ginger, peppermint or fennel** after meals for bloating.
- Eat more fibre: vegetables, fruit, beans and whole grains.
- Prioritise sleep and cut back on alcohol.

Tea can be part of a healthy routine. Just read the label and skip anything with senna unless your doctor recommends it.
