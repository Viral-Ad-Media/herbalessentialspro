Ginger (*Zingiber officinale*) is a root that has been used in Asian and Ayurvedic traditions for thousands of years. Its heat comes from compounds called gingerols and shogaols.

## Benefits backed by good evidence

**Nausea.** This is ginger's strongest area. It has been studied for morning sickness, motion sickness and nausea after surgery, often with positive results.

**Digestion.** Ginger helps the stomach empty, which can ease that heavy, overfull feeling.

**Muscle and period aches.** Some studies suggest regular ginger intake may modestly reduce period pain and muscle soreness.

**Warming comfort.** On a cold day or with a cold, a hot ginger, lemon and honey drink is hard to beat.

## Fresh ginger tea recipe

1. Thinly slice a thumb-sized piece of fresh ginger. There's no need to peel it.
2. Simmer it in 2 cups of water for 10 minutes. Simmer longer for more heat.
3. Strain, then add a squeeze of lemon and a teaspoon of honey.

Add a cinnamon stick or a pinch of turmeric for an extra-warming version.

## Who should be careful

Large amounts of ginger can cause **heartburn**. Talk to your doctor if you take **blood thinners** or **diabetes medication**, or if you have **gallstones**. Moderate amounts are generally considered safe in pregnancy, but check with your midwife.
