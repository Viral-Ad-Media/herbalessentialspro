Lemon balm (*Melissa officinalis*) is a leafy mint-family herb with a fresh citrus scent. It has been brewed since the Middle Ages and is still a favourite in herbal blends.

## 8 reasons to enjoy it

1. **It helps you unwind.** Lemon balm is traditionally used for stress and mild restlessness.
2. **It pairs well with sleep blends.** It's often combined with valerian or chamomile.
3. **It supports digestion.** Like other mints, it can ease gas and bloating.
4. **It may help focus.** Some small studies suggest calm alertness rather than drowsiness at modest doses.
5. **It soothes cold sores.** Lemon balm creams are used on cold sores. The tea is a gentler, internal companion.
6. **It's caffeine-free.** You can enjoy it at any time of day.
7. **It's easy to grow.** It spreads happily in gardens and pots.
8. **It tastes great iced.** Brew it strong, chill it, and add a slice of lemon.

## How to brew it

Use 1 to 2 teaspoons of dried leaves, or a handful of fresh leaves, per cup. Steep covered for 5 to 10 minutes.

## Who should be careful

Lemon balm may affect **thyroid medication**, so check with your doctor if you have a thyroid condition. It can add to the effect of **sedatives**. Avoid large amounts during pregnancy unless your doctor says otherwise.
