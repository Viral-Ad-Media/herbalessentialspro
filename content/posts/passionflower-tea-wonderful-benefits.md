Passionflower (*Passiflora incarnata*) is a climbing vine with striking purple flowers. The leaves, stems and flowers are dried for tea, which tastes mild and grassy.

## Why people drink it

**Calm before bed.** Passionflower is thought to raise levels of GABA, the brain chemical that helps quiet nervous activity. Small studies suggest it may improve how people rate their sleep quality, though the research is limited.

**Everyday nervousness.** Traditionally, passionflower has been used for mild, situational anxiety, such as the jitters before a presentation. It isn't a treatment for an anxiety disorder.

**A soft alternative.** Some people find chamomile too floral. Passionflower is more neutral and blends well with lemon balm or lavender.

## How to brew it

Steep 1 teaspoon of dried passionflower in hot water for 8 to 10 minutes. Drink it about an hour before bed. Start with one cup to see how it affects you.

## Who should be careful

- It can cause **drowsiness**, so don't drive after drinking it until you know how you react.
- Don't combine it with **sedatives, sleep medication or alcohol**.
- **Avoid it during pregnancy**, as it may stimulate the uterus.
- Stop it two weeks before **surgery** because of possible interactions with anaesthesia.
