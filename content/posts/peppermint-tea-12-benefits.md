Peppermint (*Mentha × piperita*) is a natural cross between watermint and spearmint. Its leaves are rich in menthol, which gives the tea its cooling bite and most of its benefits.

## 12 reasons people reach for peppermint tea

1. **It eases bloating.** Menthol relaxes the smooth muscle of the gut, which can help trapped wind pass.
2. **It can calm mild IBS symptoms.** Peppermint oil capsules have the strongest evidence here, and the tea is a gentler option.
3. **It freshens breath.** Menthol has mild antibacterial properties, and the aroma lingers.
4. **It's caffeine-free.** It's a good evening drink.
5. **It may soothe tension headaches.** The scent and the warmth help many people relax.
6. **It opens the airways.** The steam from a hot cup can make a stuffy nose feel clearer.
7. **It's refreshing cold.** Brew it strong, chill it, and you have a sugar-free iced drink.
8. **It helps you drink more water.** Flavoured, unsweetened water is still water.
9. **It may sharpen focus.** Some people find the aroma helps alertness during an afternoon slump.
10. **It's gentle after a big meal.** A cup after dinner is a long-standing habit for good reason.
11. **It's easy to grow.** Mint thrives in a pot on a windowsill, so fresh tea is always close by.
12. **It blends well.** Peppermint pairs nicely with green tea, chamomile, ginger or lemon balm.

## How to brew it

Steep 1 teaspoon of dried leaves, or a small handful of fresh leaves, in just-boiled water for 5 to 7 minutes. Cover the cup to keep the menthol in.

## Who should be careful

Peppermint relaxes the valve at the top of the stomach, so it can make **acid reflux or heartburn worse**. If you have GORD, try ginger or chamomile instead. Avoid strong peppermint products around very young children.
