Herbal teas have been used after meals for centuries, and with good reason. Warm liquid alone helps digestion, and several herbs add gentle effects of their own.

## 1. Peppermint
Relaxes the gut muscles and eases gas and bloating. Skip it if you get heartburn. [Peppermint guide](/peppermint-tea-12-benefits).

## 2. Ginger
The best-studied herb for nausea. It also helps the stomach empty after a heavy meal. [Ginger guide](/ginger-tea-health-benefits).

## 3. Fennel
Sweet and liquorice-like. Lightly crush the seeds before brewing to release their oils. It's traditionally used for wind and colic.

![Fennel tea in a glass](/uploads/2022/08/depositphotos_350095390-stock-photo-herbal-infusion-fennel-tea-glass.webp)

## 4. Chamomile
Calms both the nerves and the stomach, which helps when stress shows up as a tummy ache. [Chamomile guide](/chamomile-tea-benefits).

## 5. Dandelion root
Roasted, it tastes a bit like coffee. It's traditionally used as a bitter to stimulate digestion, and it's a mild diuretic.

## 6. Lemon balm
Another gentle mint-family herb, which is good for nervous indigestion. [Lemon balm guide](/lemon-balm-tea-enjoy-8-benefits).

## Tips

- Drink tea **after** meals rather than with them if you feel very full.
- Brew digestive herbs **covered**, so the essential oils stay in your cup.
- See a doctor about persistent pain, a change in bowel habits, weight loss, or blood in your stool.
