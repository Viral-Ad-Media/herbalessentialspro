There is no tea that melts fat. But tea can be a genuinely useful tool when you're trying to manage your weight, mostly because of what it replaces.

## How tea can help

**It replaces sugary drinks.** Swapping one fizzy drink or sweetened latte a day for unsweetened tea can save a surprising number of calories over a year.

**Green tea and matcha.** The combination of caffeine and catechins (EGCG) may slightly increase calorie burn. The effect in studies is small, around tens of calories a day at most.

**Oolong and pu-erh.** These are traditionally drunk after meals in China. The evidence for their effect on weight is limited, but they're a pleasant, low-calorie habit.

**Herbal teas for cravings.** Peppermint, cinnamon or liquorice-flavoured blends can satisfy a sweet tooth after dinner without dessert.

## What to avoid

Avoid "skinny" or "teatox" products that contain **senna** or other laxatives. They cause water loss, not fat loss, and can be harmful with regular use. [Read our detox tea guide](/detox-tea-truths-2).

## A simple routine

- **Morning:** green tea or matcha instead of a sugary coffee drink.
- **Afternoon:** oolong, or iced hibiscus with lime.
- **Evening:** caffeine-free peppermint or rooibos instead of a snack.

Pair it with protein-rich meals, plenty of vegetables, movement you enjoy and good sleep. That's where real results come from.
